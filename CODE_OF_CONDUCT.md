# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Vora community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback with empathy.
- Focus discussion on reproducible behavior and evidence.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's sensitive information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, trolling, sustained
disruption, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through
the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive incident details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and safety.
